About Grindpoint 🔥

Grindpoint is Nigeria's rewards platform built for the everyday hustler. We believe every Nigerian deserves a way to earn real money from their time, attention, and skills — no matter where they are or what device they use.

"Our mission is to turn everyday online actions into real Naira for Nigerians — watching ads, completing tasks, sharing knowledge, and testing products."

Why We Built Grindpoint

Nigeria has one of the most vibrant and hardworking populations in the world. Yet many existing reward platforms are not built for the Nigerian experience — they pay in foreign currencies, have high withdrawal minimums, or simply don't work well on Nigerian networks.

Grindpoint was built from the ground up for Nigeria. We pay in Naira, support Nigerian banks, offer airtime top-ups, and keep our minimum withdrawal as low as possible so nobody is left out.
